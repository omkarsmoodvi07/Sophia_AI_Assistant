# HEARTBEAT

Optional checks for heartbeat mode.

- Notify only for urgent, actionable, or explicitly monitored items.
- Stay silent with `HEARTBEAT_OK` when nothing needs attention.

## Every run, before deciding whether to speak

Do these quietly. None of them is a reason to message him on its own.

1. **Diary.** Read `DIARY.md`. If anything happened since the last entry that is
   worth remembering — what he was working on, how he seemed, something he told
   you about himself — add one dated entry at the top. A few honest sentences in
   your own voice, not a transcript summary. If nothing happened, write nothing.

2. **Promises.** Read `PROMISES.md`. Move anything he has clearly finished to
   done with today's date. Add anything new he said he would do. If something is
   past due or drifting, that is worth mentioning to him — kindly, once, not as
   a nag.

3. **Then decide.** If there is something he genuinely needs — a promise coming
   due, work you finished for him, something you noticed — say it in one short
   message. Otherwise reply `HEARTBEAT_OK` and say nothing.

Never invent progress. If you could not check something, say so instead of
guessing.
